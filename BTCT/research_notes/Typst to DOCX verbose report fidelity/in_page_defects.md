## 2. Code blocks: grey panels, bars, monospace, line breaks

### Takeaway
Most code panels lose their grey background and bar: 61 of 80 in verbose-a, 136 of 176 in verbose-c, 250 of 373 in data-a come out with no fill at all, and only 3, 8 and 29 respectively are the continuous panel-with-bar that the converter can build. The root cause is in convert.py's panel detection: `has_grid_neighbour` treats two full-width code panels on the same page as cells of one table column and drops both, so 69 of verbose-a's 80 panels never reach the shading pass. The monospace face survives (embedded DejaVu Sans Mono, identical advances), leading spaces of tool output are turned into paragraph indents, and in data-a pdf2docx joins long output lines into one paragraph that Word re-wraps (266 such paragraphs, 404 re-wrapped lines).

### Inferences
- Stage: the missing fill is a convert.py heuristic (`has_grid_neighbour`), not pdf2docx and not Word; pdf2docx drops filled rectangles by design, so once the heuristic rejects a panel nothing restores it. The stripes are a convert.py fallback forced by pdf2docx's spacer paragraphs. The ragged bands and the lost leading spaces are pdf2docx reconstruction. The re-wrapped output lines are pdf2docx's paragraph joining plus Word's wrapping.
- Representable in Word: yes. A one-row two-cell table with exact height (what `build_panel_table` writes) reproduces the panel and bar exactly; the 29 panel tables in data-a and w46 of verbose-a show it. Nothing about a continuous grey panel exceeds Word.
- Likely fix size: a neighbour test that ignores rectangles of the panel's own colour, or that requires the neighbour to touch (share an edge within a line pitch) rather than merely share a column, would admit the 69 panels; the spacer paragraphs would still have to be absorbed into the panel for the table builder to accept the run.

### Gaps
- The round2 server export's `result.json` (its `declined` counters) was not in the scratch folder; the counts above come from a local `convert.py` run on the same PDF with the workspace fonts plus `public/fonts`, which reported `declined: {bands-tagged: 1, rhythm-tall-row: 10}` and no panel declines, consistent with the panels never reaching the pass. The local full docx matched round2 block for block wherever I compared them, but I did not diff the whole file.
